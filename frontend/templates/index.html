Score methodology

Three perspectives, one readable score

Every component uses the same 1-to-10 scale. Scores below 4 are bearish, scores from 4 to below 6 are neutral, and scores of 6 or higher are bullish.

Premium users can adjust these weights to match their research priorities.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before signing up.

How accurate are the stock predictions? ↓

Performance depends on the model, evaluation period, and class distribution. StockLens reports balanced accuracy, macro F1, log loss, and confusion matrices where available.

Can I customize the prediction model? ↓

Yes. Premium users can adjust the weightage of technical indicators, news sentiment, and social media signals to match their personal trading strategies.

What exchanges do you cover? ↓

We currently provide real-time data and predictions for all equities listed on the NYSE and NASDAQ.

Is my data secure? ↓

Account authentication and data access are handled through Supabase with role-based application controls. Sensitive backend credentials are not exposed to the browser.
